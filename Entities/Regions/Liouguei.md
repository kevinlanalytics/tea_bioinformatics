- **Administrative area.** The gazetteer gives 六龜區 (Liouguei District) in 高雄市 Kaohsiung (City) (per [[taiwan-gazetteer]], Wikidata Q712920). Su et al. (2009) use the older name **Kaohsiung County** ("Kaohsiung Co.").
- **Name and aliases.**
  - **Liouguei**: spelling used by Jhou et al. (2024) and matching the gazetteer's English label "Liouguei District" ([[taiwan-gazetteer]]).
  - **Liukui** (alias, **assumed**): spelling in Su et al. (2009), where it is listed under **Kaohsiung Co.** among the additional herbarium specimen localities for *Camellia formosensis*, alongside Kakuhozan, Nanfong Logging Road, Nanfongshan, Shanping, and Tona. No source says that Liukui is Liouguei / 六龜區. The match rests only on romanization and the shared Kaohsiung location.
- Jhou et al. (2024) name Liouguei only through the commercial product "Liouguei tea" and give no location, county, or elevation.
- Su et al. (2009) assign the Kaohsiung County localities to the **South population** of *C. formosensis*, together with the Chiayi and Pingtung localities. Liukui itself was not one of the DNA-sampled sites, though; the Kaohsiung DNA samples came from Yuyuoshan (F-KS-1) and Sasi Logging Road (F-KS-2) (Su et al. 2009, Table 1). Neither paper places Liouguei relative to other named regions.
- Neither paper gives an elevation for Liouguei/Liukui. The Liukui specimen record in Su et al. (2009) has no elevation. The species-wide ranges (800–1,800 m per Su et al. 2009; 700–1,650 m per Jhou et al. 2024) describe the whole species, not this region, so they are not entered here. Gazetteer elevations are not used (they describe the township point, not the tea-growing range).

## Wild C. formosensis Populations

<!-- Is there documented presence of wild tea populations here? -->
<!-- Reference: Su et al. 2009 distribution map, NTU Experimental Forest reports. -->
<!-- wild-population-present: present | absent | unknown. absent needs a source saying so; no mention = unknown. -->
<!-- wild-population-evidence: dna-sample | herbarium-only | literature-mention -->

**Documented, based on a single herbarium record** (`wild-population-evidence: herbarium-only`). Su et al. (2009) list this specimen among the "Additional specimens examined" in their formal taxonomic treatment of *C. formosensis*: **Liukui, 7 Dec 1996, C. W. Huang 26 (HAST)**. Their Figure 1 marks distribution sites "based on specimens of TAI, TAIF, HAST and TNM herbaria," which places Liukui within the documented range of the species.

Limits of this evidence:
- The record is a single 1996 voucher. Neither paper says anything about the population's size, condition, or whether it still exists. Su et al. (2009) do state that wild *C. formosensis* populations in general "have drastically declined due to human disturbance."
- The specimen entry does not say whether the plant was wild-growing or cultivated. `wild-population-present: present` relies on it being part of the native distribution record in the species treatment, and on Liukui being Liouguei (assumed; see Geographic Notes).
- The Liukui plant was not sampled for the RPB2 analysis. Its placement in the South population comes from county-level grouping, not from sequence data.
- Jhou et al. (2024) do not say whether "Liouguei tea" is made from wild trees or from cultivated *C. formosensis*.

- **Liouguei tea**: Jhou et al. (2024) list it as one of the "various *C. formosensis* products … available on the market," alongside the Yuchi Farmers Association's black tea. The paper does not say what kind of tea it is (black, green, etc.), who produces it, or what plant material it is made from. It is a commercial product of the species, not a named cultivar, so it is not entered in `cultivars-grown`.

## Open Questions

- **Identity of Liukui and Liouguei.** No source says outright that Su et al.'s "Liukui" and Jhou et al.'s "Liouguei" (六龜區) are the same place; the alias is assumed from romanization.
- Elevation range: no source gives one. Coordinates are only a township point (gazetteer); no source gives the location of the Liukui specimen or of any tea-growing area.
- Is the wild population recorded in 1996 still present? What is its size and conservation status?
- What is "Liouguei tea": tea type, producer, and whether it comes from wild trees or from cultivated *C. formosensis* grown at Liouguei? Jhou et al. (2024) cite no source for the product.
- Does the Su et al. (2009) Figure 1 map mark a separate point at Liukui? The figure could not be checked in the text extraction.

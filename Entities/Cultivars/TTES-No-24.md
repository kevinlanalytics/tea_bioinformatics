# Shan Yun (TTES No. 24)

**Chinese name:** 臺茶24號 (山蘊)
**Canonical ID:** `cultivar_24`

Other names in sources:
- 山蘊 / "Shan Yun": the cultivar name on both TBRS pages ([[TTESNo24]], [[2019TTESNo]]). "Shan Yun" is the romanization used in the vault's literature notes. The TBRS pages themselves give only the Chinese name plus "Cultivar Name: TTES No. 24".
- "Yongkang tea (Taiwan Tea No. 24)": Jhou et al. 2024. Jhou does not use 山蘊 / Shan Yun, and the TBRS pages do not use "Yongkang tea" as a cultivar name (see Source Conflicts, naming).
- "TTESNo.24", sample code V024: Hu et al. 2023.

Breeding unit: the 2019 TBRS report is titled 東部分場育成臺灣原生山茶品種-臺茶24號(山蘊), i.e. a Taiwan native wild tea cultivar bred by the Eastern Branch Station (東部分場) ([[2019TTESNo]]). The station's listed phone prefix (089) and the e-mail address taitung027@tbrs.gov.tw point to Taitung; the page does not state the town. Jhou et al. 2024 credit the Tea and Beverage Research Station as a whole.

Traits, per both TBRS pages ([[TTESNo24]], [[2019TTESNo]]; identical text):
- Small-tree type (樹型小喬木型), spreading habit (樹姿橫張), strong growth vigor (生長勢強).
- Lanceolate leaves without pubescence (葉披針形不具茸毛).
- High yield (產量高); strong disease resistance and drought tolerance (抗病力及耐旱性強).
- Green and black teas made from it have aromas resembling mushroom, almond and coffee (類似菇蕈、杏仁及咖啡的香氣).

Jhou et al. 2024 describe it as "fast-growing and disease- and pest-resistant" with "a unique flavor profile". Pest resistance is not mentioned on the TBRS pages, and drought tolerance is not mentioned by Jhou. These are additions, not contradictions.

Other TBRS details:
- Three non-exclusive licensed seedling vendors (非專屬授權茶苗廠商): 臺灣菊業股份有限公司, 鴻興茶苗, 上旺茶苗 ([[TTESNo24]] only).
- Consultation experts: 蕭建興 and 余錦安 (Eastern Branch Station), 羅士凱 (Industry Service Section) ([[2019TTESNo]] only).

Molecular data: Hu et al. 2023 include TTES No.24 as V024 in the 144-cultivar TTVID 12-SSR reference set. They class it "Formosana" ("the cultivars belonging to *C. formosensis*") with Origin "Hybrid", sampled at the TRES Taitung experimental plantation. The paper reports no V024-specific result in the text found ([[huSSRbasedMolecularDiagnosis2023]]).

## Parentage Notes

<!-- Describe the breeding cross in prose. Note if sources disagree on parentage. -->

TTES No. 24 is **not a hybrid cross**. It is a selection from a single wild source:

- **TBRS ([[TTESNo24]], [[2019TTESNo]])**: the 親本 (parent) field reads 「臺灣原生山茶永康變種引種」. That is an introduction (引種) of the Yongkang variant (永康變種) of Taiwan native wild tea (臺灣原生山茶). There is no maternal/paternal pair, no accession code, and no Latin binomial. The 2019 report's title calls it a 臺灣原生山茶品種 (Taiwan native wild tea cultivar).
- **Jhou et al. 2024 ([[jhouInfluenceNaturalLight2024]])**: "the Tea and Beverage Research Station has identified *C. formosensis* from Yongkang Mountain, Taitung County as a source for breeding a fast-growing and disease- and pest-resistant cultivar". The source is framed at species level and given a locality. Jhou does not say whether it was a single tree, a clonal selection, or seedling selection.
- **Hu et al. 2023 ([[huSSRbasedMolecularDiagnosis2023]])**: Origin "Hybrid" for V024. Every V-coded TTES cultivar (V001 to V025c) carries the same "Hybrid" label, so it reads as a category for TTES-bred cultivars rather than a pedigree claim for this one. The same table puts V024 in the "Formosana" group, which fits a pure *C. formosensis* selection. The [[huSSRbasedMolecularDiagnosis2023]] and [[TTESNo24]] literature notes already flag this. Hu's Origin and group columns are a classification scheme, so they are recorded as their own rows in Known Facts and not treated as a conflict with the TBRS pedigree.

How the parent fields are filled: `parent-1` holds the source material as TBRS prints it, 臺灣原生山茶永康變種 (dropping the method term 引種). This is the framing chosen for [[TTESNo24]] under the newest-source rule (2026-11-10). Jhou et al. 2024's species-level framing, *C. formosensis* identified at Yongkang Mountain, Taitung County, is superseded on framing but is kept as the only source for the locality (see Source Conflicts). Because this is a single-source selection, not a two-parent cross, `parent-2` is blank.

**Selection method:** 引種 (introduction) is the only method term in the TBRS sources. Neither TBRS page nor Jhou describes the selection steps (single mother tree vs population, years of trials, propagation method).

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

Yes, directly, with no cultivated parent: per [[TTESNo24]], TTES No. 24 is an introduction (引種) of the Yongkang variant of Taiwan native wild tea (臺灣原生山茶永康變種).

Lineage path: 臺灣原生山茶永康變種 (Yongkang wild material; linked to [[Camellia-formosensis]] by the sources below) → introduction/selection (引種) → TTES No. 24 (named 2019).
- TBRS uses only the vernacular 臺灣原生山茶 plus 永康變種, with no Latin name. This is the framing the note adopts under the newest-source rule (2026-11-10).
- Superseded framing: [[jhouInfluenceNaturalLight2024]] identifies the source as species-level [[Camellia-formosensis]] from Yongkang Mountain, Taitung County.
- Scheme label that also ties it to [[Camellia-formosensis]]: Hu et al. 2023 put V024 in the "Formosana" group ([[huSSRbasedMolecularDiagnosis2023]]). This is a classification label, not a competing parentage claim.

Consistency with `Entities/Species/Camellia-formosensis.md`:
- That note, based on Su et al. 2009, records the **East population** at Yungkangshan, Taitung County. Specimens there were collected at 900 to 1,100 m elevation (M. H. Su 653 to 661; also S. Y. Lu 18580, 18595 at 900 m).
- In Su's RPB2 tree, that East population groups with the Nantou population, apart from the South population ([[suConfirmationCamelliaFormosensis]]).
- The species note's "Relationship to Bred Cultivars" section lists only TTES No. 18, because Su et al. 2009 predates TTES No. 24. TTES No. 24 would be a second, non-hybrid descendant. That note was not edited here.

Other Yongkang wild-tea accessions in the vault. None of these sources says any of them is the TTES No. 24 source material:
- **Hu et al. 2014** ([[huDevelopmentSTSCAPS2014]]): W8 "Yung-Kang wild tea" is coded FY (*C. formosensis* var. *yungkangensis*), "Original from Taitung", with processing suitability B,G (black, green), the same two tea types TBRS gives for TTES No. 24. Among the eight wild accessions, the largest STS/CAPS genetic distance (0.38) was between De-Hua-She wild tea and Yung-Kang wild tea. On their PCoA and UPGMA plots, F and FY together separate from the cultivated *C. sinensis*.
- **Hu et al. 2023**: four Yong-Kang wild tea accessions, W008 (No.2), W009 (No.5), W010 (No.13) and W011 (No.20), all "Formosana", "Native", sampled at Taitung. The paper does not say whether any of them is the TTES No. 24 mother material.
- **Lee et al. 2017** ([[leeDNABarcodeIdentification2017]]): Table 1 no. 102 "Yung Kang", coded (I) *Camellia sinensis*, large-leaf, "native Taiwanese wild tea". In their ITS2 trees it falls in a Taiwanese wild *Camellia* cluster with Sun Cha, Fong Huang, Mei Yuan and Min Ghai.

## Source Conflicts

<!-- Explicitly document any disagreement between sources on any field above. -->
<!-- Format: [Source A] says X. [Source B] says Y. Decision: pending/use A/use B. Reason: -->

- **Parent identity (framing of the source material).** [[TTESNo24]] and [[2019TTESNo]] say the parent (親本) is 臺灣原生山茶永康變種引種, an introduction of the Yongkang variant of Taiwan native wild tea. The vault's literature notes translate 永康變種 as "Yongkang variant" (TTESNo24 note) and "Yongkang variant/variety" (2019TTESNo note). [[jhouInfluenceNaturalLight2024]] says the source was species-level *C. formosensis* identified at Yongkang Mountain, Taitung County. Decision: use [[TTESNo24]] (2026). Reason: Newest-source rule (2026-11-10); [[TTESNo24]] is an undated TBRS page whose resource date is its capture date, 2026-08-07. Jhou 2024's species-level framing is superseded but kept in Known Facts. Caveat: the two framings may be compatible (a variant within the species), and the TBRS pages do not say whether 變種 is a formal botanical rank.
- **Classification schemes (not conflicts).** Hu 2023's genetic group ("Formosana") and Origin label ("Hybrid") for V024, Hu 2014's type code (FY, *C. formosensis* var. *yungkangensis*) for the Yung-Kang accession W8, Lee 2017's species code ((I) *Camellia sinensis*) for "Yung Kang", and Su 2009's listing of Yungkangshan specimens under *C. formosensis* without infraspecific rank answer different questions in different terms from the TBRS pedigree. Each is a separate Known Facts row, labelled with its source. Not entered in `conflicts:`.
- **Naming (not a factual contradiction).** TBRS names the cultivar 山蘊 (Shan Yun), and [[jhouInfluenceNaturalLight2024]] calls it "Yongkang tea". Both tie it to TTES No. 24 and 2019, so they refer to the same cultivar. "Yongkang tea" may be an informal or descriptive name. No source says which is official in English, although TBRS's own English field is just "TTES No. 24". Not entered in `conflicts:`.
- **Release year:** no disagreement. TBRS (ROC 108) and Jhou (2019) agree.
- **Traits:** no disagreement. Jhou adds pest resistance, and TBRS adds drought tolerance, high yield and the aroma descriptors.

## Open Questions

<!-- What do you still need to verify before flipping verified: true? -->

- **Breeding code / selection line number:** none in any vault source. It is not known whether TTES No. 24 corresponds to one of Hu 2023's Yong-Kang wild tea accessions (W008 No.2, W009 No.5, W010 No.13, W011 No.20) or to Hu 2014's W8.
- **Is 永康 a place name in the TBRS usage?** TBRS never says so. Only Jhou et al. 2024 ties the source to "Yongkang Mountain, Taitung County".
- **Selection history:** when the material was collected, whether it was a single mother tree or a population, the trial years, and the propagation method are not documented. 引種 is the only method term.
- **Taxonomic rank of the Yongkang material:** whether 永康變種 / var. *yungkangensis* is a formally published variety. Hu 2014 cites it but the vault has no protologue; Su 2009 does not recognise it.
- **Hu 2023 "Hybrid" label:** resolved for this note. It is treated as a classification-scheme label (own Known Facts row), not a parentage conflict. It is still worth confirming from the paper's methods what Hu's Origin column means.
- **Growing regions / acreage:** no source documents where TTES No. 24 is planted commercially.
- **Molecular confirmation:** no source reports a V024-specific genotype result, e.g. whether it matches a Yong-Kang wild accession or clusters with the East population.
- **Romanization:** "Shan Yun" comes from the literature notes, not from a TBRS English-language source.
- **Primary breeding report:** the 2019TTESNo item is a short web profile (title "試驗報告" but the content matches the cultivar page). A full trial or registration report with data was not found in the vault.

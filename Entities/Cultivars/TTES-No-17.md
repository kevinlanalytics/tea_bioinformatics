Other sourced attributes:

- **Status:** flagged "f", a "popular or distinguishing tea variety in Taiwan" (Lee et al. 2017, Table 1).
- **Genetic distance:** Chin-Shin-Oolong vs TTES-17 is the largest pairwise distance within the 37-accession sinensis-type group, MRD = 0.62. The group's range is 0.11 to 0.62, with an average of 0.44 (Hu et al. 2014, Results).
- **Cluster placement:** In the UPGMA dendrogram from STS/CAPS markers, TTES-17 sits in Group IIIc. Hu et al. 2014 list it among the "derived varieties" of Dah-Yeh-Oolong in that subgroup, along with TTES-2, TTES-11, TTES-16 and TTES-20.
- **Clustering in Lai et al. 2001:** "Taicha 17" clustered with "Peimaohu" in both the RAPD and ISSR dendrograms (Lai et al. 2001, Discussion).
- **Reference genotyping:** included as V017 in the 144-cultivar SSR reference database behind the TTVID kit (Hu et al. 2023, Table 1). Hu et al. 2023 do not mark it with footnote "c", which flags cultivars registered with plant variety rights.

## Parentage Notes

<!-- Describe the breeding cross in prose. Note if sources disagree on parentage. -->

**Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1):** TTES-17 is listed as "Tainon-355* (TW) x Tainon-1958 (TW)". TTES-16 (H16) has the identical cross. The asterisk points to a footnote that gives the parentage of the Tainon breeding lines:
- "Tainon-335: Dah-Yeh-Oolong x Kyang". The footnote number is **335**, not 355. No footnote entry for "Tainon-355" exists.
- "Tainon-1958: Tainon-20 x Bair-Mau-Hour"
- "Tainon-20: Hann-Koou line"

`parent-1` is set to "Tainon-355" because that is what Table 1 prints for TTES-17; it is not a judgement that 355 rather than 335 is correct. The published PDF (pages 4-5) was checked, and on 2026-11-10 its text layer was checked again: Table 1 prints "Tainon-355*" and the footnote defines "Tainon-335". The inconsistency is in the paper itself, not a conversion error in the markitdown cache or a transcription error in the literature note. `parent-2` is "Tainon-1958" as printed. Neither parent has its own entity note in the vault, so neither field is a wikilink.

**Hu et al. 2023:** lists "Tainon No.335" (H006) and "Tainon No.1958" (H008) as "Selected" accessions. It has no "Tainon No.355". It does **not** give a parentage for TTES No.17, so it neither confirms nor contradicts the cross directly.

Hu et al. 2014 do not say whether the first-listed parent is the seed parent. This note does not assume a cross direction.

If the footnote is right that the parent is Tainon-335, the pedigree from Hu et al. 2014 would be (Dah-Yeh-Oolong x Kyang) x (Tainon-20 x Bair-Mau-Hour). That would fit two other statements in the same paper: TTES-17 is classed as an SA (sinensis x assamica) hybrid, and the dendrogram discussion calls it a Dah-Yeh-Oolong-derived variety. This is a reading of the footnote, not something the paper states for TTES-17 directly.

**Classification labels and the pedigree:** Hu et al. 2014's type code SA and Lai et al. 2001's "hybrid clones between China and Assam tea" both describe a sinensis x assamica hybrid, consistent with the documented ancestry via Kyang (if the parent is Tainon-335). Hu et al. 2023 place TTES No.17 in the "Sinensis" genetic group, defined as cultivars of *C. sinensis* var. *sinensis*, while also labelling its origin "Hybrid"; their scheme has only Sinensis/Assamica/Formosana groups and no hybrid group. Lee et al. 2017's species code I gives *Camellia sinensis* without a variety. These are different classification schemes and are recorded side by side in Known Facts rather than treated as a conflict.

**Lai et al. 2001 (Discussion):** "Taicha 14", "Taicha 15", "Taicha 16", and "Taicha 17" "were developed from 'Peimaohu'". It gives no cross partner and no intermediate breeding lines. Peimaohu is described as a China-tea clone introduced from Fukien (Zuan and Shi 1995, as cited by Lai et al.). No source in the vault says whether Lai's "Peimaohu" is the same clone as Hu et al.'s "Bair-Mau-Hour", which Hu et al. 2014 name as a grandparent via Tainon-1958.

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

No source links TTES No. 17 to *Camellia formosensis*. In Hu et al. 2014 it falls in cultivated Group III (sinensis type), not Group I (*C. formosensis*). Its listed ancestry runs through Tainon lines, Dah-Yeh-Oolong, Kyang (Indian assamica), Hann-Koou and Bair-Mau-Hour. Under either footnote reading, none of these are wild Taiwanese tea. In Lai et al. 2001 it is grouped with the China x Assam hybrid clones, not with the native wild tea.

Reason: newest-source rule cannot apply — the discrepancy is internal to a single source (Hu et al. 2014), and Hu et al. 2023 only lists a 335 accession without giving TTES No.17's parentage, so it is not a competing claim. No source states the TTES-17 cross with "335" explicitly.
2. **Parentage framing.** [[huDevelopmentSTSCAPS2014]] says Tainon-355 (or 335) x Tainon-1958. [[laiAssessmentGeneticRelationships]] says "developed from 'Peimaohu'" and clusters Taicha 17 with Peimaohu in RAPD and ISSR trees. Decision: use [[huDevelopmentSTSCAPS2014]] (2014). Reason: Newest-source rule (2026-11-10). Earlier caveat: these may be compatible if Peimaohu = Bair-Mau-Hour, the parent of Tainon-1958, but no vault source establishes that identity. Relatedly, Hu et al. 2014 place TTES-17 in Group IIIc with Dah-Yeh-Oolong, while Bair-Mau-Hour falls in Group IIIa. The two marker studies would then group it differently.

## Open Questions

- **English trade name:** none found in any source. The note is titled "TTES No. 17" and `name-en` is blank.
- **Chinese name:** 臺茶17號 / 台茶17號 does not appear in any literature note or full text in the vault, so `name-zh` is blank. Lai et al.'s "Taicha 17" looks like a romanization, but no source gives the characters.
- **Year released / registered:** not stated in any source. Hu et al. 2014 give years only for TTES-19, TTES-20 and TTES-21.
- **Breeding code:** none found.
- **Tainon-355 vs Tainon-335:** which accession is the actual parent? It needs a TRES or TBRS primary record, such as an official cultivar page like the one for TTES No. 18 ([[TTESNo18]]). The same question applies to TTES-16, which has the same listed cross.
- **Cross direction:** which parent is the seed parent? Hu et al. 2014 do not say.
- **Peimaohu = Bair-Mau-Hour?** If yes, how should Lai et al.'s "developed from Peimaohu" fit with Hu et al.'s two-Tainon-line cross? Is Peimaohu a grandparent via Tainon-1958, or a different claim?
- **Hu et al. 2023 "Sinensis" label:** why does a cultivar with origin "Hybrid" and a documented assamica (Kyang) ancestor, if the parent is Tainon-335, fall in the var. *sinensis* group? Hu et al. 2023 do not explain how hybrids are assigned to groups.
- **Growing regions:** no source documents commercial cultivation areas or acreage.
- **Plant variety rights:** Hu et al. 2023 do not flag TTES No.17 as PVR-registered. Is it unregistered, or does it simply predate the Plant Variety and Plant Seed Act?

**Names and codes used in sources:**
- "TTES No.21 (TTES-21)", germplasm code H21, per Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1).
- "TTES No.21", cultivar code V021, per Hu et al. 2023 (Table 1).
- H21 and V021 are study accession codes, not breeding codes. Hu et al. 2014 also uses "H22" for FKK-1, while Hu et al. 2023 uses "H001" for "FKK No.1". The H-prefix codes in the two papers are separate numbering schemes.
- Hu et al. 2014's reference list cites the registration paper as Chiu CF, Lin JC, Huang CC, Lin JH, Shiau JH (2009) "New cultivar of black tea - TTES No.21", *Taiwan Tea Res Bull* 28:1-18 (in Chinese with English abstract). That paper is not in the vault.

**Other sourced facts:**
- Classified as a "Developed variety" with type code "AS" in Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1). Hu et al. 2023 (Table 1) groups it as "Sinensis" with origin label "Hybrid", sampled at the TRES **Wenshan** experimental plantation. The two papers use different classification schemes; each value is kept as its own row in Known Facts.
- One of the 12 "prevailing"/dominant cultivars that Hu et al. 2014 selected for variety identification. The selection criteria were acreage, suitability for a unique tea, and being newly bred. TTES-21 is grouped with TTES-19 and TTES-20 as newly bred cultivars that are "most urgently desirable for variety identification in Taiwan". Hu et al. 2014 says these 12 cultivars together "take over 98% acreage of Taiwan" (TRES statistics, 2011), but gives no per-cultivar acreage for TTES-21.
- Hu et al. 2023 does not mark V021 with footnote c (registered plant variety right), although it does mark V019, V020, V022 and V025. Hu et al. 2014 makes no plant-variety-right claim for TTES-21 either. This is an absence in the sources, not a finding that it is unprotected.

## Parentage Notes

<!-- Describe the breeding cross in prose. Note if sources disagree on parentage. -->

Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1) gives the "Origin or Parent" of TTES-21 as "FKK-1 line". The next row lists FKK-1 (H22) as a "Developed variety", code "AS", suited to black tea, from the cross **Kyang (IN) × Kimen (CN)**. In the same table, Kyang (I10) is an introduced var. *assamica* ("A") "Original from India", and Kimen (I1) is an introduced var. *sinensis* ("S") "Original from China".

So the only sourced lineage path is Kyang × Kimen → FKK-1 → (FKK-1 line) → TTES-21. Hu et al. 2014 does not say whether TTES-21 was a selection from FKK-1 progeny, a clonal selection within the FKK-1 line, or a cross involving FKK-1. The same "X line" wording is used for other single-origin entries (TTES-7 "Shan line", TTES-8 "Jaipuri line"), whereas crosses are written "A × B". This note therefore records FKK-1 as parent-1 and leaves parent-2 blank. Treating FKK-1 as a strict parent in the two-parent sense would be an inference.

Hu et al. 2023 gives no parentage for V021, only the origin label "Hybrid", which it applies to every TTES cultivar V001-V025 (including TTES No. 24, which other vault sources describe as a wild-population selection), so the label is not read here as a statement about TTES-21's pedigree. Hu et al. 2023's genetic group "Sinensis" sits uneasily with the documented lineage: the only named ancestor, FKK-1, descends from an assamica × sinensis cross (Hu et al. 2014), Hu et al. 2014 codes TTES-21 "AS", and Hu et al. 2023 itself places FKK No.1 in its "Assamica" group. Hu et al. 2023's three-group scheme has no hybrid class, so the labels are not directly comparable.

Hu et al. 2023 lists "FKK No.1" (H001) as a "Selected" cultivar in the "Assamica" group, sampled at Yangmei. Kyang (I008) and Kimen (I001) also appear in its panel as imported cultivars. Both papers place FKK-1 on the assamica side, so FKK No.1 (Hu et al. 2023) and FKK-1 (Hu et al. 2014) are presumably the same accession. Neither paper says so explicitly.

## Geographic Range

<!-- Which named regions grow this cultivar? Link to Region notes. -->

No source in the vault names commercial growing regions for TTES No. 21.

- Hu et al. 2023 sampled V021 fresh leaves at the TRES **Wenshan** experimental tea plantation (Table 1). This is a germplasm sampling site, not evidence of commercial cultivation, so it is not in `regions:`.
- Hu et al. 2014 says the black-tea cultivars TTES-18, TTES-8 and TTES-7 are mainly planted in Nantou County, and that assamica-type tea in general (about 3.9% of acreage) is mainly in Nantou County. Neither statement names TTES-21, so Nantou is not assigned here.

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

Its recorded lineage does not trace back to *C. formosensis*. Per Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1), the only named ancestors are FKK-1 and FKK-1's parents Kyang (introduced var. *assamica*, India) and Kimen (introduced var. *sinensis*, China). Hu et al. 2023 places V021 in the "Sinensis" group, not "Formosana". Hu et al. 2014 codes it "AS" (a *C. sinensis* hybrid class), not "F" or "FY".

## Molecular Evidence

<!-- Not a template section; included because the sources' TTES No. 21 content is mainly molecular. -->

- **STS/CAPS markers (Hu et al. 2014):** TTES-21 is one of the 12 prevailing cultivars identified with five core markers (M02, C02, G01, G03, G04). The Figure 1 caption says M02 separates sinensis-group from assamica-group cultivars, G03 and C02 identify the **four** assamica-group cultivars, and G03, G01 and G04 separate the sinensis group. The other 11 of the 12 cultivars are listed elsewhere in the paper: 8 sinensis (Chin-Shin-Oolong, TTES-12, Shy-Jih-Chuen, Chin-Shin-Dahpan, TTES-13, Chin-Shin-Gantzy, TTES-19, TTES-20) and 3 black-tea cultivars (TTES-18, TTES-8, TTES-7). By elimination, TTES-21 is the fourth member of the assamica group. This is my inference from the counts. The flow-chart figure itself was not legible in the text extraction.
- **UPGMA/PCoA (Hu et al. 2014):** Group II of the dendrogram contains the "A" and "AS" germplasm. TTES-21 is coded "AS", so it is expected in Group II, but the text does not name it in any subgroup.
- **SSR / TTVID kit (Hu et al. 2023):** V021 is part of the 144-cultivar reference panel genotyped with the 12-locus multiplex SSR kit. No V021-specific genotype or pairwise result is discussed in the text, and V021 was not one of the six cultivars used for primer screening.
- Lee et al. 2017's ITS2 germplasm list stops at TTES No. 20, so TTES No. 21 has no ITS2 data in the vault.

## Source Conflicts

No between-source conflicts. Hu et al. 2014's type code ("AS") and Hu et al. 2023's genetic group ("Sinensis") and origin label ("Hybrid") are different classification schemes, recorded as separate rows in Known Facts (see Parentage Notes).

- **Internal inconsistency in Hu et al. 2014 (not between sources).** The Table 1 abbreviation note defines "AS" as "C. sinensis var. assamica × var. assamica hybrid". The Figure 2 legend ("var. assamica x sinensis hybrid (AS)") and the Figure 3 caption ("var. assamica × var. sinensis hybrid (AS)") define it as an assamica × sinensis hybrid. The figure definitions match FKK-1's Kyang (A) × Kimen (S) parentage, so the table note is probably a typo, but this is not confirmed. Decision: pending.

- **Chinese name:** None of the vault sources uses 臺茶21號 or 台茶21號, and no Chinese trade name appears, so `name-zh` is blank. A sourced form is needed, e.g. from Chiu et al. 2009 or a TRES cultivar page.

- **Breeding code:** No sourced pre-release breeding-line code. H21 and V021 are study accession codes.
- **Parentage / breeding method:** What "FKK-1 line" means (selection from FKK-1 progeny, a clonal selection, or a cross with an unnamed second parent) is unresolved. Chiu et al. 2009 (*Taiwan Tea Res Bull* 28:1-18), the registration paper, should settle it and is not in the vault.
- **FKK-1 identity:** Whether "FKK-1" (Hu et al. 2014, H22, "Developed variety") and "FKK No.1" (Hu et al. 2023, H001, "Selected") are the same accession is assumed but not stated in either paper.
- **Genetic placement:** Why Hu et al. 2023 groups V021 as "Sinensis" despite its assamica-side ancestry (see Parentage Notes) is unexplained; the V021 SSR genotype or a third source would help.
- **Release details:** "Designated in 2008" comes from Hu et al. 2014 citing Chiu et al. 2009. The exact registration date, and whether TTES-21 holds plant variety rights, are unknown.
- **Growing regions and acreage:** No source names where TTES-21 is grown commercially or how much area it covers.
- **Agronomic and quality traits:** Yield, flavour or aroma, disease resistance and similar traits are not described in any vault source.

## Parentage Notes

<!-- Describe the breeding cross in prose. Note if sources disagree on parentage. -->

Hu et al. 2014 is the only source that says anything about TTES No. 6's ancestry. Its Table 1 "Origin or Parent" column lists **"Chin-Shin-Oolong line (TW)"** for H6. The paper does not explain what "line" means. Other entries use the same "X line" form, including TTES-5 ("Fwu-Jou line (CN)"), TTES-7 ("Shan line (TH)"), TTES-8 ("Jaipuri line (IN)") and TTES-21 ("FKK-1 line"). These contrast with entries written as two-parent crosses, such as TTES-19 ("TTES-12 (TW) × Chin-Shin-Oolong (TW)"). Hu et al. 2014 therefore gives no second parent for TTES No. 6. It also does not say whether "Chin-Shin-Oolong line" means a selection from Chin-Shin-Oolong, a seedling of it, or the maternal side of an unstated cross. For that reason, `parent-confidence` is set to low.

Hu et al. 2023 gives V006 the origin label **"Hybrid"** in its own classification scheme, the same label it gives its other V-coded TTES cultivars; it names no parents. That label sits uneasily with Hu et al. 2014's single-line entry ("Chin-Shin-Oolong line", with no cross partner), whereas two-parent crosses in the 2014 table are written "A × B". Neither source states TTES No. 6's breeding method outright, and the [[huSSRbasedMolecularDiagnosis2023]] literature note flags the "Hybrid" label as possibly a naming convention rather than a pedigree claim.

Hu et al. 2014 describes Chin-Shin-Oolong as a landrace (L1) "planting around Taiwan." The paper says it was among the four landraces introduced from China during the Japanese occupation period. It is also among the six landraces used as female parents in hybridization breeding since 1945. The Discussion notes that "many recently released varieties are the descents of Chin-Shin-Oolong," but it names only TTES-19 and TTES-20 explicitly. TTES-6 is not named there.

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

None of the sources connects TTES No. 6 to *Camellia formosensis*. Hu et al. 2014 gives it type code "S" (*C. sinensis* var. *sinensis*; not "SA" and not "F"), Hu et al. 2023 places it in its "Sinensis" genetic group, and Lee et al. 2017 gives species code "I" (*C. sinensis*). The only lineage given is through the Chin-Shin-Oolong landrace (Hu et al. 2014), which is a *C. sinensis* var. *sinensis* landrace.

In the Hu et al. 2014 UPGMA dendrogram, the text puts each listed derived TTES variety into subgroup IIIa, IIIb or IIIc, but it does not list TTES-6 in any of them. Its placement can only be read from Figure 3, which was not checked for this note.

- **Marker data:** Hu et al. 2023 has a TTVID SSR genotype for V006 (144-cultivar database), and Lee et al. 2017 has an ITS2 sequence for accession 6. Neither cached text reports cultivar-specific findings for TTES No. 6, so check the supplementary tables if those data are wanted.

## Geographic Range

<!-- Which named regions grow this cultivar? Link to Region notes. -->

No source in the vault names commercial growing regions for TTES No. 23.

- Hu et al. 2023 sampled V023 fresh leaves at the **Yuchi** sampling plantation (Table 1, "Sampling plantation"). Per the table footnote, the leaves came from private plantations or TRES experimental tea plantations in Wenshan, Yangmei, Yuchi and Taitung. This is a germplasm sampling site, not evidence of commercial cultivation, so it is not listed in `regions:`. See [[Yuchi]].
- Note: V023 is the only "Sinensis"-group TTES cultivar that Hu et al. 2023 sampled at Yuchi. The other Yuchi TTES accessions (V007, V018, V025) are all "Assamica". The paper does not explain this, and it should not be read as a growing-region claim.

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

Unknown. Hu et al. 2023 places TTES No. 23 in the "Sinensis" group, not "Formosana" (the *C. formosensis* group; TTES No. 24 / V024 is the only TTES cultivar in that group). With no pedigree in any source, a link to *C. formosensis* through ancestry can be neither confirmed nor ruled out.

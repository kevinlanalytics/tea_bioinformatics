## Parentage Notes

<!-- Describe the breeding cross in prose. Note if sources disagree on parentage. -->

Only Hu et al. 2014 gives any pedigree information. In its Table 1, the "Origin or Parent" column for TTES-7 reads "Shan line (TH)". For crossed varieties the same column uses the form "A × B" (for example, TTES-1 is "Chin-Shin-Dahpan (TW) × Kyang (IN)"). TTES-7 is instead listed with a single line, in the same way as TTES-5 ("Fwu-Jou line"), TTES-6 ("Chin-Shin-Oolong line") and TTES-8 ("Jaipuri line"). Hu et al. 2014 classes TTES-7 as "A" (pure *C. sinensis* var. *assamica*), not "SA" (sinensis × assamica hybrid) or "AS" (assamica × assamica hybrid). Read together, this points to a selection from the Shan line rather than a cross, but the paper does not say so in words.

Hu et al. 2014 also lists the Shan line as introduced germplasm: "Shan" (I3) and "Shan-1" to "Shan-4" (I4–I7) are all class A, suited to black tea, and "Original from Thailand". The paper does not say which Shan accession, if any, TTES-7 came from. In the same paper, the Shan-1 to Shan-4 samples came from the Tung Pang Black Tea Co. Ltd. tea garden in Nantou County, not from the TRES germplasm garden.

Hu et al. 2023 lists TTES No. 7's origin as "Hybrid". In that table, all 25 TTES-numbered entries (V001–V025) are labelled "Hybrid", so the label may be a blanket category for TTES releases rather than a statement about this cultivar's pedigree. It is a different classification scheme from Hu et al. 2014's "Origin or Parent" column, so it is recorded alongside it rather than treated as a conflict. Note that "Hybrid" sits uneasily with Hu et al. 2014's listing of a single line and its class A (rather than SA or AS); neither source explains its terms well enough to reconcile the two labels.

## Geographic Range

<!-- Which named regions grow this cultivar? Link to Region notes. -->

None of the sources says where TTES No. 7 is grown commercially. The only location given is where it was sampled: per Hu et al. 2023, the V007 reference sample came from the **Yuchi** TRES experimental tea plantation (Nantou). Yuchi was also the sampling plantation for the other assamica-group TTES entries in that table (V018, V025). This is a germplasm or experimental site, not evidence of a commercial growing region, so `regions:` is left empty.

Hu et al. 2014 sampled its material mainly from the germplasm garden at the Tea Research and Extension Station, Taoyuan County. It does not say which site the TTES-7 sample came from.

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

No source links TTES No. 7 to *C. formosensis*. Hu et al. 2014 classes it as A (var. *assamica*), not F or FY (*C. formosensis*). Hu et al. 2023 places it in the "Assamica" group, not "Formosana". The only pedigree information (Hu et al. 2014) traces it to the Shan line from Thailand, which is introduced var. *assamica* germplasm.

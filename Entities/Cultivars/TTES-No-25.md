Other sourced attributes (all from Hu et al. 2023, Table 1 and Introduction):
- **Taxonomic group:** "Assamica", which Table 1 footnote a defines as cultivars belonging to *C. sinensis* var. *assamica* (per Hu et al. 2023).
- **Origin category:** "Hybrid". Hu et al. 2023 give this label to every V-coded (TTES) cultivar, V001 to V025, including TTES No. 24, which other sources describe as a wild-population selection. Treat it as a blanket category, not as evidence of a controlled cross.
- **Legal status:** Table 1 footnote c marks V025 as "registered with plant variety rights based on The Plant Variety and Plant Seed Act, Council of Agriculture, R.O.C.". The Introduction names V019, V020, V022 and V025 as protected by the Act, "enacted in 2004 in Taiwan" (per Hu et al. 2023). Neither passage gives a registration date.
- **Sampling plantation:** the V025 leaf sample came from the TRES **Yuchi** experimental plantation (per Hu et al. 2023). This is where the sample was collected, not a stated growing region.
- **Genotyped accession:** V025 is one of the 144 cultivars in the TTVID 12-locus SSR reference database (per Hu et al. 2023). The paper reports no result specific to V025.

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

No source links TTES No. 25 to *C. formosensis*. Hu et al. (2023) place V025 in the "Assamica" group (*C. sinensis* var. *assamica*), not the "Formosana" group. That is a classification, not a lineage statement, and any *C. formosensis* contribution in its ancestry is undocumented.

**Names and codes used in sources:**
- "TTES No.8 (TTES-8)", germplasm code H8, per Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1).
- "TTES No.8", cultivar code V008 (also written "TTES No. 08"), per Hu et al. 2023 (Table 1 and Discussion).
- "TTES No.8", code 8 in the 113-variety germplasm list, per Lee et al. 2017 (Table 1).
- Not to be confused with "Tainon No.8" (code H004 in Hu et al. 2023, a selected var. sinensis line) or "Tainon-8" (a parent of TTES-12 in Hu et al. 2014). These are different entities.

**Other sourced facts:**
- Classified as a "Developed variety", assamica type ("A"), per Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1). Grouped as "Assamica", origin "Hybrid", sampled at the TRES Wenshan plantation, per Hu et al. 2023 (Table 1).
- One of 12 prevailing cultivars in Taiwan that together "take over 98% acreage of Taiwan" (TRES statistics, 2011), selected for variety identification, per Hu et al. 2014.
- One of the eight cultivars used to prescreen the STS/CAPS primer pairs, per Hu et al. 2014.
- One of six cultivars "commonly grown in Taiwan" (L001, L002, V012, V020, V008, V018) used to screen 83 SSR primer pairs, per Hu et al. 2023.
- Flagged as a "popular or distinguishing tea variety in Taiwan" (footnote f), per Lee et al. 2017 (Table 1).
- Among the 25 important, widely cultivated varieties, the highest ITS2 sequence similarity (0.984) was between TTES No.18 and TTES No.8, per Lee et al. 2017.

## Parentage Notes

<!-- Describe the breeding cross in prose. Note if sources disagree on parentage. -->

Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1) gives the origin of TTES-8 as "Jaipuri line (IN)", a single Indian assamica line rather than a cross. Its other Jaipuri-derived entries, by contrast, are written as crosses (e.g. TTES-2 and TTES-11 as Dah-Yeh-Oolong × Jaipuri, TTES-10 as Hwang-Gan × Jaipuri). Jaipuri itself appears as germplasm I9, an "Introduced variety" of var. assamica, "Original from India" (Hu et al. 2014).

Molecular data in two papers put TTES-8 very close to Jaipuri:
- Hu et al. 2014: the smallest modified Roger's distance among the 14 assamica-type accessions was 0.08, between TTES-8 and Jaipuri (tied with Shan-1 vs Shan-2).
- Hu et al. 2023: V008 (TTES No. 8) and I009 (Jaipuri) differ at only one of the 12 SSR loci, "echoing that TTES No. 08 (V008) is originated from Jaipuri (I009)."

The sources agree that Jaipuri is the parent or source line. They do not say how the cultivar was derived: by selection within the Jaipuri line, from open-pollinated seed, or by a cross. Hu et al. 2023 puts it under the origin label "Hybrid" in its own scheme but names no second parent; that label sits uneasily with Hu et al. 2014's single-line origin "Jaipuri line (IN)", and the two papers are recorded as separate scheme values rather than reconciled. Likewise, Lee et al. 2017's species code "I" (no variety) for TTES No.8 sits alongside its code "III" (var. *assamica*) for Jaipuri, while Hu et al. 2014 ("A") and Hu et al. 2023 ("Assamica") place it with assamica. No source gives a breeding code.

## Geographic Range

<!-- Which named regions grow this cultivar? Link to Region notes. -->

- **Nantou County**: Hu et al. 2014 says TTES-18, TTES-8, and TTES-7 "are mainly planted in Nantou County (estimated by Tea Research and Extension Station in 2011)." No Region note exists for Nantou County as a whole. No source names a narrower township or district (e.g. Yuchi) for TTES-8.
- The Hu et al. 2023 reference sample came from the TRES Wenshan experimental plantation. That plantation is a germplasm collection site, not a reported production region.

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

No source traces TTES-8 to *C. formosensis*. Its sourced lineage is Indian var. assamica (Jaipuri) (Hu et al. 2014, Hu et al. 2023). Lee et al. 2017 report that its ITS2 sequence is the most similar (0.984) of any pair among the 25 major varieties to that of TTES No.18. Hu et al. 2014 lists TTES No.18 as Burma × Taiwanese wild tea. This similarity is a sequence observation only. No source presents it as evidence of shared wild-tea ancestry.

## Parentage Notes

<!-- Describe the breeding cross in prose. Note if sources disagree on parentage. -->

Per Hu et al. 2014 (Table 1, "Origin or Parent" column), TTES No. 9 is the cross **Horng-Shin-Dahpan (TW) × Kyang (IN)**. Hu et al. 2014 list Horng-Shin-Dahpan as a landrace (L9, sinensis type, "Planting in north-west of Taiwan") and Kyang as an introduced assamica-type variety (I10, "Original from India").

Hu et al. 2014 do not mark which parent is female in Table 1. Their Discussion says six landraces, including Horng-Shin-Dahpan, "have been used as female parents for hybridization breeding" since 1945. That suggests Horng-Shin-Dahpan is the seed parent, but the paper does not state it for TTES No. 9 specifically.

In Hu et al. 2014's UPGMA dendrogram (Figure 3), TTES-9 falls in sinensis subgroup IIIa. The paper describes it, together with TTES-3 and TTES-4, as a "derived variet[y]" of Horng-Shin-Dahpan.

Of the three sources, only Hu et al. 2014 gives parentage. Hu et al. 2023 and Lee et al. 2017 list the cultivar without parents. Same-parent and related cultivars per Hu et al. 2014: TTES-3 and TTES-4 (Horng-Shin-Dahpan × Manipuri), and TTES-1 (Chin-Shin-Dahpan × Kyang).

**Classification labels vs. pedigree:** Hu et al. 2014's SA type code matches its documented sinensis-landrace × Indian-assamica cross. Hu et al. 2023 label V009 "Sinensis" (*C. sinensis* var. *sinensis*) in their genetic-group column while giving its origin as "Hybrid"; that genetic-group label sits uneasily with the sinensis × assamica pedigree in Hu et al. 2014, but the 2023 scheme has no hybrid genetic group. Lee et al. 2017 assign species code "I, *Camellia sinensis*" without a variety. These are different classification schemes, not competing pedigree claims.

**Transcription note:** the literature note [[huDevelopmentSTSCAPS2014]] lists this cultivar as "Horng-Shin-Dahpan × Kyang", matching the paper's Table 1 for H9 (corrected 2026-11-10 from an earlier "× Jaipuri"; "× Jaipuri" belongs to H10 and H11).

## Geographic Range

None of the sources names a production region for TTES No. 9. Known sampling locations:
- Hu et al. 2023 took the V009 sample from the TRES experimental plantation at Wenshan. This was a germplasm sampling site, not a reported growing region.
- Hu et al. 2014 drew all non-Shan samples from the germplasm garden at the Tea Research and Extension Station, Taoyuan County.
- Lee et al. 2017 sampled varieties held at the Tea Research and Extension Substation (Taitung Substation, per the literature note).

`regions` is left empty for this reason.

## Relationship to Wild Species

<!-- Does this cultivar trace back to C. formosensis? Note the lineage path. -->

None of the sources links it to *C. formosensis*. Per Hu et al. 2014, both parents are cultivated germplasm: a Taiwan sinensis landrace (Horng-Shin-Dahpan) and an Indian assamica introduction (Kyang). The same paper places TTES-9 in cultivated Group III (sinensis, subgroup IIIa), not in the wild-species Group I (*C. formosensis*). Hu et al. 2023 classify V009 as "Sinensis", not "Formosana".

Other sourced attributes (all from Hu et al. 2023, Table 1 and Introduction):

- **Botanical classification:** "Sinensis" group, i.e. *C. sinensis* var. *sinensis* (Table 1, footnote a).
- **Legal status:** Registered with plant variety rights under the Plant Variety and Plant Seed Act, Council of Agriculture, R.O.C. (Table 1, footnote c, on "V022c"). The Introduction groups V019, V020, V022 and V025 as cultivars protected by this Act, which it says was enacted in 2004 in Taiwan. The paper does not give TTES No. 22's own registration date.
- **Study accession code:** V022 in Hu et al. 2023. This is a code specific to that study, not a breeding code.
- **Origin label:** "Hybrid". Hu et al. 2023 applies this label to every V-coded TTES cultivar, including TTES No. 24, which other sources describe as a wild-population selection. The label is treated here as a blanket category, not as evidence of a two-parent cross.

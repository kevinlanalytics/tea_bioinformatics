## Abstract

Official Tea and Beverage Research Station (TBRS, 農業部茶及飲料作物改良場) cultivar profile page
for 臺茶24號 (TTES No. 24), also named 山蘊 ("Shan Yun"). The parent (親本) is given as an
introduction (引種) from Taiwan native mountain tea (臺灣原生山茶), specifically the "永康變種"
(Yongkang variant) — not a controlled two-parent cross. It was named in Republic of China year 108
(2019). The page describes a small-tree type with a spreading growth habit, strong growth vigor,
lanceolate leaves without pubescence, high yield, and strong disease resistance and drought
tolerance. Finished green tea and black tea from this cultivar are described as having aromas
similar to mushroom, almond, and coffee. It is suitable for both green tea and black tea
production. The page also lists three non-exclusive licensed tea-seedling vendors for the
cultivar.

## Key Claims Relevant to Tea KG

- 臺茶24號 (TTES No. 24) is also known by the cultivar name 山蘊 ("Shan Yun").
- Parentage (親本): 臺灣原生山茶永康變種引種 — an introduction (引種) from a Taiwan native
  mountain tea (臺灣原生山茶) population, specifically the "永康變種" (Yongkang variant), not a
  controlled two-parent hybridization cross.
- Named (命名) in Republic of China year 108, i.e. 2019.
- Tree type (樹型): small tree / arbor type (小喬木型); growth habit (樹姿): spreading (橫張);
  growth vigor (生長勢): strong (強).
- Leaf shape (葉形): lanceolate (披針形), without pubescence/down (不具茸毛).
- Yield (產量): high (高).
- Disease resistance (抗病力) and drought tolerance (耐旱性): both strong.
- Suitable tea types (適製茶類): green tea (綠茶) and black tea (紅茶).
- Sensory quality: green tea and black tea made from this cultivar have aromas similar to
  mushroom (菇蕈), almond (杏仁), and coffee (咖啡).
- Three non-exclusive licensed tea-seedling vendors (非專屬授權茶苗廠商) are listed: 臺灣菊業股份
  有限公司 (Taiwan Ju-Ye Co., Ltd., 0938-765075), 鴻興茶苗 (Hongxing Tea Seedlings, 0932-575001),
  and 上旺茶苗 (Shangwang Tea Seedlings, 0985-232898).

## Methods Used

- Germplasm introduction/selection (引種): collecting and propagating a wild tea population
  (永康變種 of 臺灣原生山茶) rather than performing a controlled hybridization cross.
- Morphological characterization: tree type, growth habit, growth vigor, leaf shape/pubescence.
- Agronomic evaluation: yield, disease resistance, drought tolerance.
- Tea manufacturing trial: processing into green tea and black tea.
- Sensory/quality evaluation: aroma characterization of the finished teas.
- Official cultivar naming/registration procedure (品種命名), completed in 2019 (ROC year 108).
- Commercial licensing: non-exclusive propagation-rights licensing to named tea-seedling vendors.

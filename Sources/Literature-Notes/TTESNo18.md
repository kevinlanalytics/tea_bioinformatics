## Abstract

Official Tea and Beverage Research Station (TBRS, 農業部茶及飲料作物改良場) cultivar profile page
for 臺茶18號 (TTES No. 18), also named 紅玉 ("Red Jade"). Bred by TBRS from a cross of maternal
parent 緬甸大葉種B-729 (Myanmar large-leaf type "B-729") and paternal parent 臺灣野生山茶B-607
(Taiwan wild mountain tea "B-607"), officially named in Republic of China year 88 (1999). The page
describes it as an upright-arbor, large-leaf, early-maturing type with elliptical mature leaves,
pale yellow-green buds with sparse hair and no white down, strong growth vigor, medium-strong
disease/pest resistance, and strong drought tolerance. It is suited for black tea production,
yielding a slightly coarse leaf strip, black color with a purple sheen, bright golden-red liquor,
and a rich, mellow, fresh taste with subtle cinnamon and mint aroma notes; it is described as one
of the main cultivated varieties for black tea in the Yuchi (魚池), Nantou area.

## Key Claims Relevant to Tea KG

- 臺茶18號 (TTES No. 18) is also known by the cultivar name 紅玉 ("Red Jade").
- Parentage (親本): maternal parent (母本) 緬甸大葉種B-729 — Myanmar large-leaf type, accession
  code "B-729"; paternal parent (父本) 臺灣野生山茶B-607 — Taiwan wild mountain tea, accession
  code "B-607". This is a more specific accession-level pedigree than the generic "Burma ×
  Taiwanese wild tea" cross recorded elsewhere in the vault (see Conflicts section).
- Officially named (正式命名) in Republic of China year 88, i.e. 1999.
- Tree type (樹型): upright arbor/tree type (直立型喬木); large-leaf type, early-maturing variety
  (屬大葉型、早生種).
- Mature leaf shape (成葉): elliptical (橢圓型).
- Bud color (茶芽芽色): pale yellow-green (淡黃綠色); bud pubescence sparse, no white down
  (茶芽茸毛稀，不具白毫).
- Growth vigor (樹勢): strong (強).
- Disease/pest resistance (抗病蟲害能力): medium-strong (中強).
- Drought tolerance (抗旱性): strong (強).
- Suitable tea type (適製): black tea (紅茶).
- Finished tea characteristics: leaf strip slightly coarse (葉條索稍粗); color black with a purple
  sheen (色澤墨黑泛紫光); liquor color bright golden-red (茶湯水色金紅鮮明); taste rich, mellow,
  fresh, and smooth (滋味濃醇鮮爽); aroma carries subtle cinnamon and mint notes (香氣中帶有淡淡
  肉桂及薄荷香氣).
- Production note: one of the main cultivated varieties for black tea in the Yuchi (魚池) area of
  Nantou County (南投魚池地區紅茶主要栽培品種之一).

## Cultivars / Species Mentioned

- 臺茶18號 (紅玉 / "Red Jade" / TTES No. 18) — the focal cultivar of this page.
- 緬甸大葉種B-729 — Myanmar large-leaf type, accession "B-729"; maternal parent of TTES No. 18.
- 臺灣野生山茶B-607 — Taiwan wild mountain tea, accession "B-607"; paternal parent of TTES No. 18.

## Regions Mentioned

- 南投魚池 (Yuchi, Nantou County) — main production area for black tea made from TTES No. 18.
- Myanmar (緬甸) — origin of the maternal parent, "B-729" large-leaf type.

## Methods Used

- Controlled cross-breeding (雜交育種): maternal × paternal cross of two named accessions
  (B-729 × B-607).
- Morphological characterization: tree type, growth habit, leaf shape, bud color/pubescence.
- Agronomic evaluation: growth vigor, disease/pest resistance, drought tolerance.
- Tea manufacturing trial: processing into black tea.
- Sensory/quality evaluation: leaf appearance, liquor color, taste, and aroma characterization.
- Official cultivar naming/registration procedure (品種命名), completed in 1999 (ROC year 88).

## Abstract

This Tea and Beverage Research Station (TBRS, 農業部茶及飲料作物改良場) profile page documents the cultivar 臺茶24號 (TTES No. 24), also given the name 山蘊 ("Shan Yun"), a new tea variety developed by the Station's Eastern Branch Station (東部分場, in Taitung). Rather than resulting from a controlled hybridization cross, the cultivar was developed by introducing and selecting from a population of Taiwan native wild tea (臺灣原生山茶), specifically the "永康變種" (Yongkang variant/variety); it was officially named in 2019 (Minguo 108). TTES No. 24 is described as a small-tree (小喬木) type with a spreading growth habit (樹姿橫張) and strong growth vigor, lanceolate leaves without pubescence (葉披針形不具茸毛), high yield, and strong resistance to disease and drought. It is suitable for processing into both green tea and black tea, both of which are reported to exhibit aromas reminiscent of mushroom, almond, and coffee. The page also lists TBRS contact experts (at the Eastern Branch Station and the Industry Service Section) available for further consultation on the cultivar.

## Methods Used

<!-- Molecular method, survey method, breeding record, etc. Relevant for provenance tagging. -->

- Germplasm introduction/clonal selection (引種): collecting and propagating a wild tea plant/population (永康變種 of 臺灣原生山茶) rather than performing a controlled cross-pollination breeding cross.
- Morphological characterization: assessment of tree type (樹型), growth habit (樹姿), growth vigor, and leaf shape/pubescence.
- Agronomic evaluation: assessment of yield, disease resistance, and drought tolerance.
- Tea manufacturing trials: processing the cultivar's leaf into finished green tea and black tea.
- Sensory/quality evaluation: aroma characterization of the finished teas (noting mushroom, almond, and coffee notes).
- Official cultivar naming/registration procedure (品種命名), completed in 2019.

## Conflicts With Other Sources

- **[[huSSRbasedMolecularDiagnosis2023]] mislabels TTES No.24's origin type.** That paper's cultivar table groups V024 (TTES No.24) under "TTES-bred hybrid cultivars" ("Hybrid" origin, by V-code convention). This report and [[jhouInfluenceNaturalLight2024]] agree that TTES No.24 (山蘊/Shan Yun) instead came from direct introduction/selection of a single wild *C. formosensis* population (永康變種/Yongkang variant) — not a two-parent hybridization cross. Treat this report as authoritative on TTES No.24's pedigree/origin type.
